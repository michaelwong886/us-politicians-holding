📜 Legal Basis

The STOCK Act (2012) requires all members of Congress and their spouses to disclose securities transactions over $1,000 within 45 days via Periodic Transaction Reports (PTRs). The president files via the OGE (Office of Government Ethics).

⚠️ Limitations

  • 45-day lag — trades are always delayed, not real-time
  • Self-reported — accuracy depends on compliance
  • Amount ranges — law only requires ranges (e.g. $1,001–$15,000), not exact values
